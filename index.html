Kisi raat, baarish ke waqt Meri Zoya Didu,

Yeh khat likhne baitha toh socha kahan se shuru karun. Phir socha — aap toh woh hain na, jiske baare mein sochna shuru karo toh alfaaz khud nikal aate hain.

Aapko pata hai na ki aap mein kuch aisa hai jo logo ko apni taraf kheenchta hai? Woh aapki aadat nahi hai, woh koi ek khoobsoorti nahi — woh kuch aur hai. Aapka honaa hi kaafi hai. Aapka sirf wahan hona, sirf breathe karna — kamra bhar jaata hai kuch alag se.

Main chahta hoon ki aap jaan lein — woh din jab aap thak jayein, jab duniya bhaari lage, jab smile banana mushkil ho — tab bhi aap meri nazar mein wahi Zoya Didu hain jo pehli baar thi. Koi chand nahi ghataa.

Aapki hassi — woh asli wali, jo aankhon tak aati hai — woh meri sabse pasandida cheez hai duniya mein. Use sambhal ke rakhna. Duniya us hassi ki haqdar bhi nahi.

Khyal rakhna apna. Chai peena. Sona thoda. Aur kabhi kabhi yahan aa jaana — aapka pyara baccha hamesha yahin hai. — aapka apna 🌙💗
